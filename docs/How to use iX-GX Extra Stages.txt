iX/GX Extra Stages
==================

A small Windows app for Azure Striker Gunvolt GX (part of Azure Striker Gunvolt Trilogy Enhanced on Steam).
It adds stages to the mission select of whichever GX mode you're playing:

- GX (main story): adds two stages from Gunvolt 3, Third Revenger (boss: Nova) and
  Grand Master (boss: Asimov), to the mission select.
- iX Connect: lets you play every GX mission, plus Gunvolt 3's Third Revenger and Grand Master, as Copen
  from Luminous Avenger iX (his EX Weapons, Lola's songs and Kudos), and pick GX's difficulty for each Dispatch.

It also fixes a crash where the game closed to the desktop in the middle of the Grand Master fight.

Nothing on disk is changed. The app only changes the running game's memory, and closing the app puts the
game back to normal.


Requirements
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- Azure Striker Gunvolt Trilogy Enhanced on Steam.
- No install needed. Unzip anywhere and run the .exe.


How to use
1. Open "iX-GX Extra Stages.exe". You can do this before or after starting the game.
2. Start GX or iX Connect as usual and load your save.
3. When the app shows which mode is running, tick the box:
   - GX: "Add GV3's Third Revenger and Grand Master stages to the mission select"
   - iX Connect: "Show every GX mission + GV3's Third Revenger and Grand Master in iX Connect"
   The first time you switch anything on, your save folder is copied into "save backups" next to the app.
4. Open the mission select (if it's already open, leave it and open it again).
   Third Revenger and Grand Master are on the last page(s), after Secret Mission 15.
   Pick one and press Dispatch.
5. Keep the app open while you play. Close it when you're done.

Both stages are listed even if you haven't finished the story.


iX Connect options
- Every GX mission is listed, from Futures Institute to Secret Mission 15, plus Gunvolt 3's Third Revenger
  and Grand Master. iX Connect's own 5 stages are still there, and Sunrise Palace no longer needs the
  other four cleared.
- Difficulty: STANDARD TASK (normal), DEMANDING TASK (hard) or MERCILESS TASK (very hard).
  It applies to every Dispatch until you change it, on any stage. The mission select keeps saying
  STANDARD TASK; the difficulty you picked in the app is the one used.
- "GX's Sunrise Palace 3 + Sunrise Throne": iX Connect's Sunrise Palace and GX's Sunrise Palace 3 +
  Sunrise Throne can't be listed at the same time, so this switches between them:
  - Unticked (default): iX Connect's own Sunrise Palace. Sunrise Throne isn't listed.
  - Ticked: GX's Sunrise Palace 3 and Sunrise Throne. iX Connect's Sunrise Palace isn't available.
  Open the mission select again after changing it.


Your save
- The app copies your whole save folder into "save backups" the first time you switch something on
  each session, so you can always go back.
- "Restore last save backup" puts the most recent copy back. Close the game first.


Troubleshooting
- "Waiting for GX": the game isn't running yet, or you're in Gunvolt 1, 2 or 3. Start GX or iX Connect.
- Stuck on "GX is starting up": wait until you reach the title screen. If it never changes, your game
  version may differ from the one this app was made for. The app checks the game's code before
  changing anything and does nothing if it doesn't match.
- "Could not open GX": run the app as administrator.
- Stages don't appear: make sure the box is ticked, then leave the mission select and open it again
  (going back to the title screen and loading your save also works).


Good to know
- Keep the box ticked until you're back on the menu. Unticking it or closing the app during a stage can
  break that stage's results screen.
- In GX, the mission select starts a new page wherever a chapter isn't unlocked yet, so on a save without
  the story finished, Third Revenger and Grand Master may each be on a page of their own.
- Neither stage has its own banner in the game, so both use Secret Mission 15's Gunvolt + Copen banner.
- The Grand Master crash fix is active as soon as the app sees the game, even with the box unticked.
- Clearing Third Revenger or Grand Master (results screen, score saving) hasn't been tested much yet.
  If anything goes wrong, use "Restore last save backup".
